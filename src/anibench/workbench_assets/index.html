THE COMPARISON CONTRACT

Every percentage has a question.

A score measures attainment of a fixed set of tasks. It is never the percentage of human biology understood.

What counts as a task?

A named quantity, population, time window, required observations, and precision target. For example: estimating a baseline population mean within a declared standard error.

What does 100% mean?

All of the selected category's weighted tasks meet their requirements under the chosen assumptions. Other categories can still fail. Exact level attainment is checked separately.

What does a range mean?

Confirmed passes form the lower bound. Unresolved task weight can extend the upper bound. A range across variance scenarios expresses model sensitivity; neither range is automatically a statistical confidence interval.

What cannot buy a score?

More files, duplicated assays, money spent, publication status, or an ethics label. Independent people, valid timing, complementary measurements, and identified comparisons matter through their measurement model.

Equations and assumptions

For fixed positive task weights w and task pass indicators a, attainment is 100 × Σ(w a) / Σw. Unknown tasks remain in that denominator.

For an estimable scalar with sampling variance v and target variance τ², the secondary precision progress is min(1, τ² / v). It is not a probability of success.

The conditional linear model uses J = HᵀR⁻¹H. Observation operators and covariance must have source-supported semantics. A strong prior does not become information collected by the study.

The design explorer uses the preserved v0.4 hypothetical model. Population, individual change, randomized contrast, and platform comparison use distinct sampling models. AB2 inherits AB1 targets with tighter precision and additional questions. Native targets and exact windows are visible in each design result.

Publication and ethics filters

These controls select visible studies; they do not change scores. Unknown approval is distinct from reported non-approval. A registry entry is not proof of peer-reviewed results or ethics approval.
