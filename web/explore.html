03 / A REPRODUCIBLE STANDARD

Inspect the reasoning.
Change the experiment.

AniBench is a proposed benchmark and an open implementation. Software verification, source review, and biological validation are separate milestones.

01

Specify the observation

Who was measured, when, with which assays, and how accurately? Linked participant-events, observation operators, and covariance describe what the study can resolve. Modality names alone earn no points.

02

Keep learning distinct

Depth, total information, trajectories, causal contrasts, adaptive decisions, and transport remain separate. Planned and realized geometry retain their claim classes. Neither gets a maturity bonus.

03

Make progress measurable

Compare each native metric on an identical basis. A Pareto frontier contains designs for which no compared alternative is at least as good on every objective and strictly better on one. Future levels increase resolution without rescaling previous results.

The information model and its limits

For a declared local observation model, information is F = Σ n AᵀR⁻¹A across independent event groups. Prior-whitened information G gives local log-volume contraction L = ½ log₁₀ det(I + G). This is a model-dependent capacity measure, not a measured quantity of biological understanding. Correlation, redundancy, noise, missingness, and linkage matter. Nonlinear biology and empirical predictive utility require separate validation.

Movement requires a dynamical model: sampling frequency and elapsed time alone do not prove that biological trajectories can be predicted. Perturbation requires an identifiable intervention contrast. Personalization requires evidence that learning an individual improves a prediction or decision.

Native metrics have no winner-normalized ceiling. Capacity comparisons require common code, authority, parameter-space, and metric definitions. A hash binds bytes; it does not establish scientific truth or independent review.
